   - title: "닫기 (ESC)"

5. **Image element** (click does NOT close):
   - `max-w-full max-h-full object-contain`
   - onClick: `e.stopPropagation()` to prevent overlay click
   - alt: "확대된 운동 사진"

Follow existing Fable component patterns (module declaration, open statements, ReactComponent attribute).
